## Example Consulting Group

### Engagement Manager

#### Legacy Platform Assessment

Led a three-month assessment of a client's legacy order-management
system, interviewing stakeholders across engineering, support, and
finance to map out where the system's technical debt was actually
costing the business money versus where it was merely inconvenient.

Produced a prioritized modernization roadmap that the client's board
approved in full, including a phased migration plan designed so each
phase shipped independent, measurable value rather than requiring the
whole rewrite to land before anything improved.

#### Client Relationship

Managed the relationship with three concurrent clients across the
engagement, each with different risk tolerances and reporting needs —
adapted communication style and cadence per client rather than using one
template for all of them.
