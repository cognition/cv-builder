## Moe's Tavern

### Regular / Unofficial Mood Officer

#### Stakeholder Rapport

Built lasting relationships across Springfield’s most demanding
stakeholder environment — a stool, a begrudging bartender, and a jukebox
that has heard every complaint twice.

Became the unofficial focal point for after-hours problem solving, from
bowling strategies to “what does this yellow sludge mean” consulting.

#### Incident Soft-Landing

Specialized in de-escalating evenings that started with a small mistake
and were headed toward a town-wide parade of consequences.
